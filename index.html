The Vision

Building the Future of Toledo Athletics

The Champions Complex renovates and reimagines the Health Education Building into 74,000 square feet built around one idea: every Rocket, in all 17 sports, gets what they need to win, in the classroom and in competition, under one roof in the heart of campus.

It is one of the department’s biggest priorities because it serves everyone: academics, nutrition, wellness and training together, steps from where Rockets practice.

The Champions Complex is a vital commitment to our student-athletes. It guarantees they have the elite resources needed to achieve competitive excellence and succeed in every aspect of their lives. Tom Moreland, VP and Director of Athletics

Gifts to the Champions Complex are among the most visible and lasting investments in Toledo Athletics history. This is your opportunity to leave something for Toledo.

8:00 AM Training Table

Breakfast at the Training Table

Nutrition is performance. Athletes gather for chef-prepared, sport-specific meals that accelerate recovery and fuel the full demands of a student-athlete's day.

Morning Study Session

Between classes, the Academic Center provides a focused environment for studying, tutoring, and advisor meetings — all steps away from the practice field.

The Training Table is more than a dining hall — it's where team culture is forged. Midday meals bring squads together, fueling both the body and the bond between teammates.

2:30 PM Indoor Turf

Afternoon Practice

The indoor turf facility means preparation never stops — rain, snow, or shine. Year-round access to a full-scale training surface keeps Rockets sharp every day of the year.

The day closes with preparation for tomorrow. State-of-the-art meeting rooms with film review capabilities let coaches and athletes break down every detail of their performance.

The Impact

Transforming Lives, Building Champions

The Champions Complex will change the daily life of every one of our 450+ student-athletes and bring baseball and softball home to main campus from Scott Park.

  • Centralized academics, nutrition, health, wellness, and training under one roof
  • State-of-the-art nutrition center supporting peak athletic performance
  • Dedicated academic support spaces with tutoring resources
  • Year-round indoor training facility for multi-sport use
  • Modern team meeting spaces with film review and presentation capabilities
  • Returns baseball and softball to main campus, fostering greater team unity
